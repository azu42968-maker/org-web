About Acid

What is ACID?↓

ACID is a Brawlhalla organization built around competitive esports, giveaways, content, and community.

What does ACID bring that other organizations don’t?↓

ACID focuses heavily on the community and content side of esports. We provide opportunities for creators, players, editors, artists, and community members to grow together.

What does ACID provide?↓

ACID provides tournaments, events, coaching, content opportunities, creator support, video editing, clipping programs, partnerships, giveaways, and opportunities to work with the organization.

How successful is ACID competitively?↓

ACID has competitive players across different skill levels and continues to build its competitive roster. — Wamuu

What is ACID’s origin?↓

ACID's origin traces back to an idea Shiesty had on a 2K game server. Shiesty and Jp built the server together, and Shiesty continued growing it into what ACID is today.

Joining Acid

What are ACID’s Brawlhalla Elo requirements?↓

Our general competitive requirement is 2,000+ Elo. Certain roles, teams, or opportunities may have different requirements. — Wamuu

What benefits does joining ACID provide?↓

Members can access community events, tournaments, coaching, content opportunities, giveaways, creator programs, partnerships, and opportunities to work with ACID.

Can I volunteer or work for ACID?↓

Yes. ACID has opportunities for staff, moderators, media, recruitment, events, and other positions.

Apply through the Acid Discord ↗

Can GFX artists join ACID?↓

Yes. We welcome talented GFX artists who want to contribute to ACID.

Apply through the Acid Discord ↗

Can content creators join ACID?↓

Yes. Content creators are a major part of ACID. We provide opportunities to grow, collaborate, and receive support.

Apply through the Acid Discord ↗

Programs & opportunities

Does ACID offer coaching?↓

Yes. ACID offers coaching and educational content designed to help players improve.

Does ACID host tournaments or events?↓

Yes. ACID regularly works on tournaments, community events, challenges, money matches, and other activities.

Does ACID have video editors?↓

Yes. ACID has video editors and media staff who help create content for the organization and its creators.

What are ACID Clippers?↓

ACID Clippers are members who help find, clip, and submit entertaining or important moments that can be turned into ACID content. — Wamuu

How do I submit clips to ACID?↓

Join the ACID Discord and submit your clips through the ACID Content Program.

Open the clip submission channel ↗
Email acidshiesty@gmail.com ↗

Will I receive credit if ACID uses my clip?↓

Yes. When ACID uses your clip, proper credit will be given to the creator whenever possible. — Wamuu
